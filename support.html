Support

Email us: ennhila.apps@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

TikTok login isn’t working

Tap Reload on the login page. If a “Continue with” option won’t open, log in with your email, phone or username instead. Check that TikTok itself works and that your connection is stable.

Why are some reposts missing?

Reposts of videos that were deleted or made private don’t appear in TikTok’s list, so the app can’t count or remove them.

Some reposts came back

TikTok sometimes restores a few. The app checks every cleanup after 24 and 48 hours, and removing them again is free.

Why is there a daily limit?

Going slowly keeps your account safe. Free gives you 10 removals a day, plus more for inviting friends. Plus goes up to 1,000 a day.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions. To get Plus back on a new phone, tap Restore on the upgrade screen.

Delete your data

In the app: Settings → Account and data → Delete account and data. See the Privacy Policy.
