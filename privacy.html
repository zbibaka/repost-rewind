Privacy Policy

Repost Rewind · Effective 9 November 2026

The short version. Your TikTok login, session and reposts stay on your phone. We never see your TikTok password. If you choose to sign in to Repost Rewind, we store your email and an account ID so your rewards and purchases follow you. No ads, no tracking, no selling data.

Who we are

Repost Rewind is made by Ennhila Apps. Contact: ennhila.apps@gmail.com. Repost Rewind is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance.

What stays on your phone

  • Your TikTok session. You log in on TikTok’s own page inside the app. The session cookies are kept by your phone’s web view and are never sent to us.
  • Your reposts and cleanup history. The list of reposts, your keep rules, what was removed and when, and verification results are stored only on your device.
  • How removal works. When you tap remove, the app asks TikTok to remove those reposts from your own logged-in session, on your phone, the same way you would one by one. None of this passes through our servers.

We don’t collect your TikTok password, TikTok messages, contacts, location, photos or browsing history. If you save a cleared card to Photos, the app only adds that image; it never reads your library. Reminders about your cleanup are scheduled on your phone; we don’t use push notifications.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), servers in the EU (Paris).
  • RevenueCat (subscription status).
  • Expo (app updates). The app checks Expo’s servers for bug-fix updates, which shows them your IP address, app version and platform.
  • Apple (payments, Sign in with Apple) and Google (Sign in with Google), under their own privacy policies.

We use no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don’t sell or share your data for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. On-device data is kept until you disconnect TikTok, delete the app, or delete your data in the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings → Account and data → Delete account and data. This removes your account, rewards and invite records from our servers and clears the app’s data on your phone.
  • Export your data: Settings → Account and data → Export my data.
  • Disconnect TikTok: Settings → Account and data → Disconnect TikTok logs you out inside the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California), you can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing ennhila.apps@gmail.com. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription; manage that in your Apple ID settings.

Children

Repost Rewind is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
